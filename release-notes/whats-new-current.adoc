== Novus initial release

NetApp Novus combines the scalability of parallel NFS (pNFS) with the proven data management capabilities of ONTAP, providing a high-performance storage solution for AI and other data-intensive workloads. It's designed for leading-edge throughput and low latency to meet the requirements of customers with exascale computing and gigawatt-scale AI factories.
